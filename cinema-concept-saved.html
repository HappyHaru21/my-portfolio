Beyond static snapshots: gated recurrent units and graph attention networks for smarter multi-agent traffic control

Mohita, S. D. Singh, D. Subitha, J. C. Kavitha

Existing multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) approaches often fail to address dynamic spatio-temporal congestion propagation across arterial intersections or accommodate sensor data failure.

This paper introduces HC-STGRL (Hybrid Coordinated Spatio-Temporal Graph Reinforcement Learning), fusing Gated Recurrent Units for temporal memory, Graph Attention Networks for adaptive spatial messaging between intersections, and 3DQN for signal phase decisions.

Evaluated on SUMO microsimulation under varying sensor failure rates. All improvements statistically significant (p<0.01).

Table 1. SUMO Benchmark Results

Travel Time −11.3%
Waiting Time −14.9%
Sensor Faults Stable @ 20%

All metrics relative to CoLight baseline on 4×4 arterial grid.
